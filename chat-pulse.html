              Which words should Pulse Chat watch for?

              When chat says one of these, it's marked on the graph and in the chat, and live streams can alert you. Any word works: your name, a teammate, a game, a running joke.

              One word each, up to 8. A message counts when one of its words starts with yours. Watching "pog": "pog", "@Pog" and "POGGERS" all match, "apogee" doesn't.

              Download clips as MP4?

              To save a clip or a whole VOD as a video file, Pulse Chat uses ffmpeg, a free video tool. It isn't included with the app, so it's fetched once from its official builds and checked before use.

              ffmpeg for this computer

              Press Transcribe on a VOD and Pulse Chat turns its audio into text, on this computer. Nothing is uploaded. You can search the words, starred clips get titles from what was said, and "Loud reactions" ranks moments by how loud they were.

              Tick the VODs to transcribe; they run one after another with the model and language set in the Transcript card, and each is saved as it finishes. Leave the tab open (the computer awake); you can keep using Pulse Chat meanwhile. Stop ends the queue, and what was done is kept.
